# BC Creative Digital — Brand & Tone of Voice

The official guide. Apply the colours (below, in theme.json) and this voice to all site copy.

## Colours (WCAG 2.2 AA tuned)
- **Electric Blue** `#1A3BFF` — primary. Buttons, links, key UI, brand fills.
- **Lime Green** `#A3FF1A` — accent. CTAs, highlights, key takeaways. Use sparingly. **Dark text only on lime — never lime text on white.**
- **Deep Navy** `#0B0F2A` — body text, dark headers, dark cards.
- **Pure White** `#FFFFFF` and **Light Grey** `#F4F5F8` — backgrounds and breathing room.

Every text/UI pairing in the theme clears WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1 text, 3:1 large/UI).

## Tone of voice (UK English)
We speak to business owners who want results, not jargon — a knowledgeable, helpful partner, completely confident but easy to chat to over a cup of tea.

**1. Friendly & approachable** — warm, conversational, everyday UK English. Use contractions. "Let's get your website working harder."
**2. Confident & bold** — short, assertive, punchy. No vague or passive phrasing. "Be on page 1. Page 2 is where businesses get forgotten."
**3. Easy to understand** — benefit over complexity. Explain any technical term by its result. "A pretty website is nice. A website that works is better."

**Slogan / attitude:** No jargon. Just results.
**CTAs:** direct, low-risk, inviting — "Get a free website review", "Let's have a chat", "Is your SEO working?"

### This, not that
- "Get found on Google" — not "Optimise your search engine presence"
- "Get more enquiries" / "Get your website working harder" — not "Unlock your brand's growth potential"
- "Big ideas. Bold digital." / "We build websites that work" — not "We provide bespoke digital design solutions"
- "DM us for a free website review" / "Let's have a chat" — not "Inquire today for a consultation"
